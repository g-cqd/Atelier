# Diary — 2026-10-18 (02)

## Remove the compatibility modules

Work on `chore/remove-compatibility-modules`, based on the extraction in PR g-cqd/Atelier#4.
Six shim products and targets removed: `KittyTerminal`, `KittyStyle`, `KittyCodecs`, `KittyInput`,
`KittyRenderer`, `AtelierText`. Hundreds of imports and three qualified type references now name
TerminalKit. `AtelierTextRendering` and `AtelierTextView` remain app modules.

Removed four alias-identity tests: mechanically rewriting them made their assertions tautological.
App integration tests remain. Removed duplicate canonical dependencies introduced by the migration.
Updated an opt-in parser corpus path that still named the extracted style source.

Before the final audit, all three release builds passed. KittyCode reported 970 tests before
removing its four alias tests; GitDiffViewer reported 1,161 tests. AtelierCore reported 1,628 tests.
Final verification and landing are recorded below.

## Allocation measurement: observation, not a proven fix

One full AtelierCore run failed its existing lexer allocation ceiling: 7 against a bound of 4.
Three filtered runs and three subsequent full-suite runs passed with the original 64 samples.
An experimental increase to 512 samples also passed four times, but that does not prove a stable
measurement or establish the cause. The increase was reverted to keep this import migration scoped.
The original ceiling remains unchanged. A separate follow-up should isolate allocation measurements
and validate the counter semantics; more minimum-of-repeats samples do not guarantee isolation.

## Interrupted builds

Earlier test commands stopped during compilation without diagnostics. A standalone release build
completed in about 220 seconds, and later suites completed. Lock contention and tool/process
termination were hypotheses, not demonstrated causes. Missing diagnostics alone do not identify
why a process exited.
